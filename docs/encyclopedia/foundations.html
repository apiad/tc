Foundations

The numbers this universe runs on, and the constraints it may not break.

This entry is the root of the encyclopedia. Everything else derives from it, and nothing in it derives from anything else. Its companion is guide-master-plan, which holds the why; this file holds the how much and the what may not happen.

How to use this file

Constants are cited, never restated. Every number in the Transcendent Chronicles lives in exactly one place — the table below — and every other entry refers to it by ID. An entry that writes out a figure instead of citing it will drift the first time the figure changes, and the figures have changed once already.

Invariants are falsifiable. They are not themes or preferences. Each one can be checked against a claim and can be shown to be violated. An entry that contradicts an invariant is wrong, not interesting.

Entries declare what they rest on in their frontmatter:

derives_from: [I-04, C-01, propulsion]

bin/tc-check validates that every cited ID exists, that the derivation graph has no cycles, that no entry restates a guarded value without citing its constant, and that every named world orbits a star in lore/data/nearby-stars.csv. The star catalog is the one place where this encyclopedia is checkable against something outside itself: the F/G/K rows and their habitability judgements come from Sol Company’s ten-parsec tables, and the M-dwarf rows from a SIMBAD query for everything with a parallax above 100 milliarcseconds — which is exactly the ten-parsec boundary.

The Guard column names a literal string the checker watches for. If an entry contains that string without citing the constant that owns it, the check fails. Constants whose values appear too often in ordinary prose are left unguarded rather than made noisy: 30 ly and 0.2c are ordinary measurements, and C-07’s bare ~150 and C-10’s bare ~250 collide with durations — the Expansion wave takes ~150 years and the Synthetic Wars run ~250. Where a figure is guardable but its digits are not distinctive, the guard carries a word of context — ~80 locked rather than ~80. A guard that fires on innocent prose is worse than no guard, because a noisy gate is one people learn to route around.

On the emptiness of the neighbourhood

I-08 says the diaspora met nobody. It does not say the galaxy is empty, and no entry may treat the one as evidence for the other.

Orion’s reach is C-01 — a bubble of C-06 good systems inside a galaxy of C-21 stars. At its height the Confederacy held some two thousand systems, roughly one part in a hundred million of the whole. Finding nobody in a sample that size is not evidence of absence. It is not evidence of anything.

The galaxy may be crowded. The only others humanity ever found were itself.

What is not here

Dates and eras live in galactic-timeline. The calendar systems live in stellar-calendar. The star catalog is lore/data/nearby-stars.csv, which is data rather than prose and is not an encyclopedia entry.
